# Side-profile sourcing

A season reference sheet that lists the combinations game by game shows which drawings belong to which combination. Match the modeled combination to a game entry before using its drawings. The [Broncos' Week 2 announcement](https://www.denverbroncos.com/news/broncos-to-wear-sunset-orange-jerseys-summit-white-pants-vs-jaguars) confirms Sunset Orange jerseys, Summit White pants, orange socks and navy metallic satin helmets against Jacksonville on September 20, 2026.

Official release galleries, such as the [Broncos Mile High Collection gallery](https://www.denverbroncos.com/photos/a-closer-look-at-the-broncos-new-mile-high-collection), supply physical side, front, back and detail photos for measurement and verification. They are never warped into textures. The [Broncos design explanation](https://www.denverbroncos.com/news/an-inside-look-at-the-broncos-uniform-design-process-for-the-mile-high-collection) identifies the mountain-peak sleeve cap, contrasted sun, triangle-cluster helmet stripe, summit-marker collar tag and 5280 pant mark. It does not provide enough geometry to invent a logo path.

Infer only in this order: symmetry, stripe continuity across visible panels, mirrored verified decals, garment construction, then reuse of a measured template. Do not infer number or name placement from a side view, a logo shape from prose, or any decal that has not been seen. Record uncertainty in the kit TODO list and leave unsupported marks absent.

## Ten-point side checklist

1. The shoulder panel continues from front across the cap to back without a UV seam or disconnected patch.
2. The shoulder bands and the peak agree with a side release photo in position, relative size and edge direction. Check band colors on the modeled jersey itself: Denver's three jerseys share the band shapes but rotate the colors.
3. The pants-side color order and widths match the selected 2026 game reference; check both legs.
4. The stripe runs continuously along the outer seam from hip to hem without jumping to the front.
5. Helmet side art stays within the shell silhouette and clears apertures and hardware.
6. The helmet front bumper is legible from the front and does not land on the crown.
7. Rear bumper text and the rear number are legible from the back when specified.
8. Front and back numbers and the name read in the correct direction; mirrored art must stay anatomically placed.
9. The collar mark and sleeve details match their observed positions, including any under-sleeve marks.
10. Base-color textures contain no baked shadows or highlights; rendered lighting comes from the material and scene.

The Broncos horse is the archive's three-layer decal, placed by a box measured on the gallery's helmet side photo. The triangle clusters are two strips either side of the crown's centre line, running up the back, per the gallery's detail photo and a season reference sheet's centre-stripe swatch; their size and spacing are estimated. The pant stripe order, the slanted end of the orange stripe and the 5280 position come from a season reference sheet's white-pants side panel. The kit's TODO list records what remains estimated.
